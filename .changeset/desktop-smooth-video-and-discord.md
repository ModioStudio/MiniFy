Stop the desktop app from freezing on track changes: Discord presence now talks to Discord on its own thread, honours the saved setting at startup, is reported by the main window only and shows the track while paused. Music videos no longer show captions, YouTube's title bar or play animation, cost less while playing, and the Discord and other switches stay in sync between the mini player and the desktop window. The dragged panel sizes are now also saved in the settings file.
