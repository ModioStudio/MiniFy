---
"MiniFy": patch
---

Play the song's music video in the desktop app, either in the side panel instead of the cover or behind the main view. Videos are found on YouTube automatically, play muted and in sync with Spotify, and show none of YouTube's controls. An optional YouTube sign-in in Settings uses the account for the search and, with YouTube Premium, removes ads; without it, ads stay hidden behind the cover until the video starts.
